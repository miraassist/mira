# Alarm – spec

Status 2026-11-22: decided, nothing built. The signal that ends the ringing is checked on the Show; the other rows
of the state table are tried in step 1. Scenarios: [alarm.feature](alarm.feature).

## Goal

"Weck mich morgen um 6" works at every satellite, not only on a TECHO5: today the skill `wecker` calls the device's
`alarm_set` ([Skill actions](skill-actions.md)), and a satellite without alarms of its own answers "Hier kann ich
keinen Wecker stellen". And an alarm can wake with more than a ring: "Stell für morgen einen Wecker mit SWR3 und mach
langsam das Licht an".

## The concept it hangs on

- **Scheduled actions** ([Scheduled actions](scheduled-actions.md)): an automation in Home Assistant that Mira builds,
  the integration checks (`check_actions`) and the service saves; it runs while the Mira service is off; listing,
  extending and cancelling happen in the conversation. The alarm is such an automation, with its own label, as it
  rings until someone reacts and may repeat on days.
- **Mira's sounds** ([Sounds and cards](satellite.md)): files in the Mira service's binary, served by the integration
  at the open path `/api/mira/panel/ui/sounds/<name>.mp3`, played over Home Assistant's internal address. The wake
  sound is two more of them.

Departures, with the reason:

- [Skill actions](skill-actions.md), "Deliberately not": "an alarm melody via the media player as fallback: left out".
  It is no fallback any more but the one way at every satellite; decision 3 (2026-11-22) replaces that line, and the
  skill `wecker` no longer calls the device's `alarm_set`.
- The scheduled actions' automations run once (`mira_once`); an alarm may repeat (`weekdays`), so it is not deleted
  after it ran and carries a label of its own, `mira_alarm`.

## Decided (2026-11-22, the maintainer)

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At which satellites | At every satellite that can take an announcement (`assist_satellite` with the feature `ANNOUNCE`: ESPHome, Wyoming and VoIP satellites in Home Assistant 2026.11.1). One without it: Mira says it cannot wake there |
| 2 | How it rings | `assist_satellite.announce` with `media_id` = the wake sound, `preannounce: false` |
| 3 | One way or two | One way: Mira's alarm at every satellite, a TECHO5 too. The model calls one tool. Not chosen: the device's alarm on a TECHO5 (with `alarm_wake_with` since TECHO5 v1.6.47: only stations from the device's list and Music Assistant favorites, the light is the screen, not the room; two ways that behave differently); both at once as a safety net (two alarms to keep in step). What the device's alarm has and Mira's has not: it rings without Home Assistant and without the network, snooze, it shows on the screen. A user who wants that sets it at the device |
| 4 | The wake sound | One sound, no choice: the marimba motif C-E-G-A-G-E (sample 4 of 2026-11-22), a rise from -30 dB to full over 20 s, then a loop of 10.4 s at full; generated by Mira's own script, so no licence of others. Who wants something else says "mit SWR3" or "mit meiner Playlist" |
| 5 | How long it rings without a reaction | At most 10 minutes, then it stops; nothing else happens |
| 6 | What counts as a reaction | The satellite goes to `listening` while it rings (the wake word). Checked on the Show (below). A stop word that ends the announcement is a reaction too, not checked: the Show's stop word is English only and misses a German "Stopp" (TECHO5's own to-do) |
| 7 | The volume | Before it rings, the alarm sets the satellite's speaker to 90 % (`media_player.volume_set`, `0.9`), so that a volume turned down or muted the night before does not make it quiet. Essential, not optional |
| 8 | The volume after the alarm | Back to what it was before the alarm |
| 9 | A satellite whose volume cannot be set (no `media_player` of its device, as a Wyoming satellite) | No alarm there: without a volume it can rely on it is no alarm. Mira says so when asked to set it |
| 10 | Radio | Plays until it is stopped, as radio; no 10-minute bound |
| 11 | Light | Rises before the alarm, so that it is full at the alarm time |
| 12 | Home Assistant off at the time | The alarm is not rung later: only the time trigger, no trigger on Home Assistant's start (the variant with the least logic) |
| 13 | An alarm that could not ring | The automation reports it, and the person gets a message |
| 14 | Asked without a satellite (Telegram, the companion app) | Nothing is set; Mira says that she sets an alarm only at a satellite, in the room where it is to ring. Not chosen: asking for the room (more logic) |
| 15 | Deleting an alarm while it rings | The volume goes back as after a reaction: the tool does not delete a ringing alarm at once but fires the event `mira_alarm_cancel` with the automation's id; the run stops the round (`media_stop`), sets the volume back and ends; then the tool deletes it |

## What it does

### Setting

"Weck mich morgen um 6" at a satellite: the tool `alarm` with `time`, `days` (empty: once), and optionally
`station` (radio) and `light` (on, slowly).

1. Mira finds the satellite of the turn, its area and its speaker (the `media_player` of the satellite's device).
   Without a satellite (Telegram, a companion app): nothing is set, the tool says so (decision 14).
2. Mira builds the automation (below) and saves it through the integration (`mira/alarm/build`, as
   `mira/once/build`, admins only), labelled `mira_alarm`, the person and the area in its variables.
3. Mira says what is set: time, day, room, and what it wakes with ("Wecker steht: morgen, 6 Uhr, mit SWR3.").

What it costs: one integration call to build and check, one to save, one reload of automations; with a station, one
search in Radio Browser or Music Assistant at setting time (the station is written into the automation, not searched
at 6 in the morning). The turn waits for all of it (about a second, not measured).

### The automation

One run does everything in order, so that it keeps what it read at the start (the volume) to the end:

1. It reads the speaker's volume and keeps it.
2. It checks that the speaker can be reached; if not, it sends the message of decision 13 and ends.
3. It sets the speaker to 90 % (decision 7).
4. It plays the wake sound round by round: `media_player.play_media` with `announce: true` on the satellite's
   speaker (first the rise, then the loop). After starting a round it waits for one of two signals: the satellite
   goes to `listening` (a reaction) or the speaker goes back to `idle` (the round is over). A reaction ends the
   rounds; so do the 10 minutes (decision 5).
5. After a reaction it waits until the satellite is `idle` again (Mira has answered), then sets the volume back to
   what it read in 1 (decision 8). Without a reaction it sets it back after the last round.

The light has a trigger of its own, a few minutes before the alarm time (decision 11).

```yaml
alias: "Wecker Schlafzimmer 1a2b3c4d"
description: "Mira: Wecker Schlafzimmer"
variables:
  mira_title: "Wecker Schlafzimmer"
  mira_time: "06:00"
  mira_days: ""            # once; or weekdays, mon,wed
  mira_owner: anna
  mira_area: schlafzimmer
  mira_kind: alarm
triggers:
  - trigger: time
    at: "06:00:00"
    id: ring
  - trigger: time          # only with light
    at: "05:50:00"
    id: light
conditions: ...            # on its days
mode: single
actions:
  - choose:
      - conditions: "{{ trigger.id == 'light' }}"
        sequence:
          - action: light.turn_on
            target: { area_id: schlafzimmer }
            data: { brightness_pct: 100, transition: 600 }
      - conditions: "{{ trigger.id == 'ring' }}"
        sequence:
          - variables:
              before: "{{ state_attr('media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker', 'volume_level') }}"
              until: "{{ now() + timedelta(minutes=10) }}"
          - if: "{{ states('media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker') in ['unavailable', 'unknown'] }}"
            then:
              - action: mira.send_message
                data: { receiver: anna, text: "Dein Wecker um 6 Uhr im Schlafzimmer konnte nicht klingeln." }
              - stop: speaker unreachable
          - action: media_player.volume_set
            target: { entity_id: media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker }
            data: { volume_level: 0.9 }
          - repeat:
              sequence:
                - action: media_player.play_media
                  target: { entity_id: media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker }
                  data:
                    media_content_id: "{{ <wake_rise> if repeat.first else <wake_loop> }}"
                    media_content_type: music
                    announce: true
                - wait_for_trigger:
                    - trigger: state
                      entity_id: assist_satellite.schlafzimmer_satellite
                      to: listening
                      id: reaction
                    - trigger: state
                      entity_id: media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker
                      to: idle
                      id: round_over
                    - trigger: event                 # decision 15
                      event_type: mira_alarm_cancel
                      event_data: { automation: "{{ this.attributes.id }}" }
                      id: cancel
                  timeout: "00:01:00"    # safety net only: a round is 24 s or 10.4 s
              until: "{{ (wait.trigger and wait.trigger.id in ['reaction', 'cancel']) or now() >= until }}"
          - if: "{{ wait.trigger and wait.trigger.id == 'cancel' }}"
            then:
              - action: media_player.media_stop
                target: { entity_id: media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker }
          - if: "{{ wait.trigger and wait.trigger.id == 'reaction' }}"
            then:
              - wait_for_trigger:
                  - trigger: state
                    entity_id: assist_satellite.schlafzimmer_satellite
                    to: idle
                timeout: "00:02:00"      # safety net: a turn that never ends
          - action: media_player.volume_set
            target: { entity_id: media_player.schlafzimmer_speaker }
            data: { volume_level: "{{ before }}" }
```

- **Why one run**: the volume read at the start is a variable of the run, and nothing else has to know it. Not chosen
  (the first draft): the reaction as a second trigger with `mode: restart`; its run would not know the earlier
  volume, and keeping it elsewhere (`scene.create`) is refused by the integration's check (an action without a
  target).
- **Why `play_media` and not `assist_satellite.announce`**: `announce` returns only when the round is over, so the
  run could not watch for the reaction during it; `play_media` returns at once. Every alarm satellite has a speaker
  (decision 9).
- **The round that is playing** plays to its end on the Show after the wake word (seen 2026-11-22 with `announce`);
  "Stopp" as a command stops it.
- **Radio** instead of the wake sound: the station plays on the room's player (Music Assistant's where there is one,
  as [Music with Music Assistant](music-players.md), part 4; else the satellite's speaker, as `radio_play`), without
  rounds: it plays until it is stopped (decision 10). The volume is set on that player and not set back (no end to
  wait for).
- **Light** (decision 11): the lead (`light_lead`, ten minutes) is a constant of the code, not a setting.
- **Once** (`mira_days` empty): after it rang, the service deletes it as it deletes a finished `mira_once`.
- **Home Assistant off at the time** (decision 12): only the time trigger; an alarm whose time passed while Home
  Assistant was off does not ring.
- **An alarm that could not ring** (decision 13): the speaker unreachable at the time (above), or a round that never
  starts playing (the sound not fetched: the speaker does not go to `playing`; how the run sees that is tried in
  step 1). The message goes by `mira.send_message` to the person.

### Listing, changing, deleting

"Welche Wecker habe ich?" lists the person's alarms: the automations with `mira_alarm`. "Lösch den Wecker", "Nein,
halb sieben" (`thread_undo`) work on them. Alarms set at a TECHO5 itself are the device's and not listed. Details with
step 3.

Interrupting and cancelling: a reaction ends the ringing (rows 4 and 5); "Lösch den Wecker" before the time deletes the
automation; deleting it while it rings ends the run (2026-11-22, fake Show: the round that played ran to its end, no
next round, and the volume stayed at 90 %). So the tool cancels a ringing alarm first (decision 15): the event
`mira_alarm_cancel` stops the round at once and the run sets the volume back (2026-11-22, fake Show: 0.2 again, the run
ended within half a second); then it deletes the automation.

## Errors

| Where | What fails | What happens | Who learns of it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setting | the satellite has no `ANNOUNCE` | nothing is set | the user: "Hier kann ich keinen Wecker stellen." |
| Setting | the satellite has no speaker whose volume can be set | nothing is set (decision 9) | the user: "Hier kann ich keinen Wecker stellen." |
| Setting | the integration is missing or refuses (`NotAllowed`) | nothing is set | the user ("Den Wecker konnte ich nicht stellen"), the log with the refusal |
| Setting | the station is not found | nothing is set; Mira asks for the station | the user, as `radio_play` |
| Setting | saving or reloading fails | nothing is set | the user and the log, with Home Assistant's answer |
| Ringing | the speaker is unreachable at the time | nothing rings; the run ends | the person, by a message (decision 13); the automation's trace |
| Ringing | the sound cannot be fetched (internal address wrong) | the speaker does not go to `playing` | the person, by a message, once step 1 shows how the run sees it |
| Ringing | Home Assistant is off at the time | the alarm does not ring (decision 12) | nobody: there is no one to tell it. Assumption to name in the guide |
| Cleanup | deleting a finished once-alarm fails | it stays; tried again at the next change, as `mira_once` | the log |

## Steps

1. **Try on the test HA** the automation above with the fake Show and once with the Show (with sound, said
   beforehand): every row not yet checked, the deleting while it rings; radio by Music Assistant (row 10). Fill in
   the state table. Failure: a row that does not hold changes this spec before anything is built.
2. **The wake sound** in the binary: `wake_rise`, `wake_loop`, the script that renders them next to them, a line in
   the sounds' licence file.
3. **The tool `alarm`** (`set`, `list`, `delete`) and `mira/alarm/build` in the integration; the skill `wecker` calls
   the tool instead of its skill action. The tool's description tells the model the fields and that the room is the
   satellite's.
4. **Radio and light** (decisions 10 and 11).
5. **The guide** (both languages): "Weck mich" at every satellite, with radio and light; that an alarm set at a TECHO5
   itself is the device's own.
